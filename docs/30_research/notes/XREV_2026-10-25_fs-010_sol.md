## Pass 1 — sol's answer, verbatim

## Factual errors

- Quoted sentence: “Oracle Financial Services Analytical Applications (OFSAA) data model.”
  What is wrong: OFSAA is an application suite, not the name of a single industry data model. Treating the suite itself as the proprietary peer conflates applications, their shared data foundation, staging structures, and application-specific analytical schemas.
  Correction: Name Oracle Financial Services Data Foundation (FSDF), including its Common Staging Area and results/analytical structures, as the relevant model/foundation within OFSAA; describe OFSAA as the suite that uses it.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “IBM Banking & Financial Markets Data Warehouse (formerly BDW).”
  What is wrong: “Formerly BDW” erases the Financial Markets lineage. IBM Banking and Financial Markets Data Warehouse brought together/evolved IBM’s banking and financial-markets warehouse model offerings; it is not adequately described as merely a rename of Banking Data Warehouse.
  Correction: Describe it as the successor/combined offering derived from IBM Banking Data Warehouse and IBM Financial Markets Data Warehouse, while noting that IBM naming has varied by release.
  Confidence: Medium.

- Quoted sentence: “Teradata describes FSDM as covering 400+ broad subject areas.”
  What is wrong: This almost certainly confuses a published count of another kind—such as entities, business concepts, or use cases—with subject areas. An industry model may contain hundreds or thousands of entities, but “400+ broad subject areas” is not a credible characterization of the FSDM’s subject-area decomposition and is release-sensitive in any event.
  Correction: Remove the number unless it can be tied to a named FSDM release and Teradata’s exact counting unit; report subject areas, entities, and attributes separately.
  Confidence: Medium.

- Quoted sentence: “ECB BIRD (Banks' Integrated Reporting Dictionary). Free. This is the nearest analogue. It has a Logical Data Model, an Input Layer, and transformation rules to regulatory outputs.”
  What is wrong: BIRD is an integrated regulatory-reporting dictionary and transformation framework. Its input-layer model is designed backward from specified statistical and prudential reports; it is not a broad operational financial-services integration model comparable in scope to FSDM. “Logical Data Model” also overstates BIRD as a conventional enterprise logical model rather than a reporting-oriented data dictionary/model with input, transformation, and output artefacts.
  Correction: Describe BIRD as a freely accessible, reporting-oriented input data model and rules corpus that is useful for the regulatory-reporting slice of a bank. Do not call it the nearest overall FSDM analogue without restricting the comparison to regulatory reporting.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “ISO 20022 data dictionary / business model. Free. A repository of business components, oriented around payment and securities messages.”
  What is wrong: ISO 20022 is not limited in orientation to payments and securities. Its repository also covers domains including trade services, foreign exchange and cards. In addition, “free” is too broad: the online repository and message definitions are freely accessible, but ISO standards documents and their copyright/licensing position are not thereby universally free or open-licensed.
  Correction: Say that the ISO 20022 repository and published message definitions are freely accessible and span payments, securities, trade services, FX, cards and related financial messaging; do not characterize the entire standard as open or free without qualification.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “These are ordered from closest to furthest from an FSLDM-style integration layer.”
  What is wrong: The order does not follow the stated criterion. Silverston supplies logical entity-relationship patterns and is structurally closer to a warehouse logical model than ISO 20022 messages, BIAN’s service architecture, or the EBA’s reporting metamodel. BIAN’s cross-bank business-object semantics can also be closer to an enterprise conceptual layer than a message repository, depending on whether breadth or implementable schema is being measured. Accessibility appears to have influenced the order even though the sentence claims that architectural distance alone determines it.
  Correction: Either remove the total ordering or define separate comparison axes: enterprise breadth, operational-versus-reporting scope, logical-schema form, semantic depth, and implementation readiness. On schema form alone, Silverston belongs ahead of the messaging, service-landscape, and reporting-output artefacts; on regulatory-reporting fitness, BIRD and EBA DPM belong near each other.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “Combining BIRD's model for structure with FIBO for definitions comes closest to what FSLDM provides, without licensing.”
  What is wrong: BIRD plus FIBO does not supply the breadth, integrated operational entity model, keys/relationships, product and agreement coverage, mappings, or implementation collateral of FSDM. The two artefacts also use different scopes and modelling paradigms, so combining them requires substantial design and semantic alignment. “Without licensing” is false literally: both are distributed under terms of use or licences, even if no proprietary Teradata licence fee is required.
  Correction: Say that BIRD and FIBO can contribute regulatory-reporting structure and financial semantics respectively, but do not constitute a drop-in or scope-equivalent FSDM replacement; they avoid a proprietary FSDM licence, not licensing altogether.
  Confidence: High.

## Important omissions

- Quoted sentence: “omission”.
  What is wrong: The Financial Industry Business Data Model (FIB-DM) is omitted. It is an openly available logical/conceptual financial-industry data model derived substantially from FIBO and expressed in a form much closer to conventional data architecture than the OWL ontology alone. It is the most direct counterexample to calling BIRD the nearest public analogue.
  Correction: Add FIB-DM, state its FIBO-derived provenance, distinguish its conceptual/logical model from a production-ready physical warehouse schema, and verify the licence of the particular repository/release used.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “omission”.
  What is wrong: The FINOS Common Domain Model (CDM), originating as the ISDA CDM, is omitted. It is openly available and models financial products, trades, lifecycle events and processes in a machine-executable domain model. Its scope is capital-markets transactions rather than an enterprise warehouse, but it is a major public industry model a financial-services data architect would expect to see.
  Correction: Add FINOS CDM with its product/trade/event/process scope and explicitly state that it is a transactional domain model, not a bank-wide integration schema.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “omission”.
  What is wrong: FpML is omitted. It is a publicly available, long-established data standard and object/message model for OTC derivatives and related trading processes. If ISO 20022 is included despite being message-oriented, excluding FpML leaves a material capital-markets gap.
  Correction: Add FpML as a domain-specific public standard for derivatives products, confirmations, reporting and lifecycle messages; place it alongside ISO 20022 rather than presenting it as an enterprise integration model.
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “omission”.
  What is wrong: The FIX Protocol’s public repository/model, including FIX Orchestra artefacts, is omitted. It supplies machine-readable semantics and workflows for electronic trading and is at least as relevant to the capital-markets part of financial services as ISO 20022.
  Correction: Add FIX/FIX Orchestra as a trading-message and workflow model, with its access and licence terms qualified by artefact/version.
  Confidence: Medium.

## Unverified items

- Quoted sentence: “The exact licence is **(unverified)**; it was MIT at last check.”
  What is wrong: For the EDM Council’s published FIBO ontology source repository, MIT is the correct licence. That does not automatically relicense every OMG specification document, EDM Council website page, trademark, or third-party content associated with FIBO.
  Correction: State: “The FIBO ontology source is released under the MIT License; separately published specification documents and marks retain their own terms.”
  Confidence: High.

- Quoted sentence: “Download terms (registration) are **(unverified)**.”
  What is wrong: There is no single registration rule covering all BIAN artefacts. BIAN has historically required a free account/login for some full Service Landscape releases and portal downloads, while public website material, API definitions, and some code repositories can be accessed without that registration. Membership is not generally required merely to inspect the public standard.
  Correction: State: “Some packaged BIAN Service Landscape downloads require free portal registration; other public artefacts, including API/repository material, are available without it. Check the terms for the exact release and artefact.”
  Confidence: Medium.

verdict - FAILS + the omitted open FIB-DM is closer to an FSLDM-style logical integration model than the document’s claimed nearest analogue, BIRD

## Pass 2 — sol's answer, verbatim

## Findings

- Quoted sentence: “**FIB-DM (Financial Industry Business Data Model).** A conceptual/logical data model derived from FIBO, in conventional data-modelling form. Of the models listed here, it is closest in form to an FSLDM-style logical model. Its licence and editions are **(unverified)**; check before use.”

  The qualification does not prevent the entry from collapsing two materially different offers under one name. Jayzed Data Models Inc. publishes and holds the copyright in FIB-DM. The publicly downloadable **FIB-DM Core** is a partial model derived from the FIBO 2018/Q4 Foundation, Business Entities, and Finance, Business & Commerce modules; Jayzed reports 1,029 entities and licenses it under **GNU GPL-3.0**. Download requires a form and delivery link. The **full FIB-DM** is a separate commercial edition, transformed from the current quarterly FIBO production release and licensed under the proprietary **Jayzed Intellectual Property License Agreement (IPLA)**; customers with maintenance receive updates. Therefore “publicly accessible alternative” applies to the older Core, not to the current full model. The upstream FIBO ontology is MIT-licensed, but that does not make either FIB-DM edition MIT-licensed. [Jayzed terms of use](https://jayzed.com/terms-of-use/), [Core download](https://fib-dm.com/data-model-download/), [full-model comparison](https://fib-dm.com/full-data-model-upgrade/), and [Jayzed licence explanation](https://jayzed.com/fib-dm-open-source-license-and-copyright/) support these distinctions. Confidence: **high** for publisher, copyright holder, edition split, and licences; **high** for the Core's FIBO vintage and 1,029-entity scope; **medium** for the full edition's exact current entity count because Jayzed's current pages say both 3,176 and 3,177 while identifying the same 2026/Q2 source release.

  Jayzed's IPLA defines FIB-DM as a **conceptual data model**, and the download page calls the supplied PowerDesigner artefact a Conceptual Data Model. “Conceptual/logical” consequently overstates the publisher's classification. “Conceptual enterprise data model in conventional data-modelling form” is supportable; “closest in form” can remain if it is explicitly a comparison of modelling form rather than public scope, currency, or implementation readiness. Confidence: **high**. [Jayzed IPLA](https://jayzed.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Jayzed-IPLA.pdf), [FIB-DM open-source page](https://fib-dm.com/open-source/).

- Quoted sentence: “**Oracle Financial Services Data Foundation (FSDF).** This is the data model underneath Oracle's OFSAA application suite.”

  The official acronym is **OFSDF**, not FSDF. Oracle also describes OFSDF as an analytical data-warehouse platform/application pack containing logical, staging, and reporting data models plus supporting infrastructure, so calling the whole foundation “the data model” is imprecise. A precise correction is: “**Oracle Financial Services Data Foundation (OFSDF).** Within the OFSAA family, OFSDF supplies the logical model and the staging and reporting physical data models used as a common analytical data foundation.” Confidence: **high**. [Oracle OFSDF introduction](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E92917_01/PDF/8.1.x.x/8.1.2.0.0/FSDF_HTML/UG/2_Introduction_to_OFSDF.htm), [Oracle relationship to OFSAA](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E92917_01/PDF/8.0.9.0.0/FSDF_HTML/UG/Relationship_to_Oracle_Financial_Services_Analytical_Applications.htm).

verdict - FAILS + the publicly available FIB-DM is the older GPL-3.0 Core, while the current full model is a separate commercial IPLA edition
