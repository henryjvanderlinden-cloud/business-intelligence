THE DATA PLATFORM — run by deterministic machinery

  • A Data Vault, or a functionally equivalent layer, holds all data and all history, integrated on business keys.
  • Rule versioning and lineage: any past report can be reproduced.
  • Temporal behaviour and join knowledge are measured, and tested before anything relies on them.
  • No model operates in the data path. Proposed: the machinery keeps running when the agentic layer is down; its checks run on their own schedule and raise their own alerts.

THREE GATES — run by the machinery, not by agents; accepted, not yet formally decided

  • Change gate: code reaches the machinery only when its tests pass.
  • Load & publication gate: a mart version is published only when its tests pass. Plausibility checks warn; an error of ten times or more against like-for-like blocks publication until someone looks.
  • Semantic admission: an approved rule or definition starts feeding marts only through this gate.
  • Only people may override a gate, and every override is recorded. Passing the gates is necessary, not sufficient, before agents work unattended.

TESTING

  • Tests are sorted by the root cause each is meant to catch first.
  • Release regression on frozen data: every change in the output must match a release note, or it is a regression to fix.
  • Plausibility checks are a quick, daily sanity check on what comes in and how it is transformed.

PRINCIPLES

  • Mandate is separate from capability; models never operate in the data path; autonomy is tiered.
  • Every accepted claim traces to evidence or to a responsible person.
  • Where machines can check the output, the agentic layer scales; where only judgement can check it, people remain the bottleneck.
  • Human attention is the scarce resource. Where damage is bounded, reversibility plus fast detection can substitute for review; more review is not always less risk.
  • Cost must not simply move elsewhere.
  • Generic: the full design is described; an implementation starts lean and adopts each further stage only once it needs it and its prerequisites work.

WHAT A PILOT WILL PROVE

  • The central bet: can the agentic layer propose business keys well enough to be worth a person's time to check?
  • The economics: cost per proposal, and human minutes of adjudication per week. Neither is quantified yet.
  • What success means for each capability.
  • Whether a team of agents does better than one builder agent.
  • Under way: a sandbox on a simulated order system (Wide World Importers), advanced one simulated day at a time. Business owners and reviewers are simulated there, and results must say so.
